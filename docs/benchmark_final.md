## Root Cause: Why ElasticNet Wins

1. **Weak nonlinear signal**: GB performs worse than linear regression (gap = -0.20)
2. **Multicollinearity**: arrival_month/arrival_week_number at r=0.995
3. **Overfitting**: GB generalization gap = 0.689 vs EN = 0.014
4. **Feature redundancy**: Trees split redundantly on correlated features
